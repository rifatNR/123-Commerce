About the APP:
    - This is a simple e-commerce app. (that has regular delivery products but mostly third party delivery products that will be used as drop shipping. So we'll sell products that we don't have in stock and will be delivered by a third party.)
    - This app is mostly for bangladeshi customes.
    - Will mostly used for ordering products to user who comes from Facebook ads (or other social media ads).
    - The products of the app can come from different sources, so the app should be able to handle multiple product sources.
    - User can only add to card, place orders no payment integration is needed. The payment and delivery will be handled offline by the seller. (will discuss the flow latter.)



App Flow:
    - 2 type of user: Admin and Customer.
    - Customer comes from social media ads and can browse products, add them to the cart, and place orders.
    - Admin will see that bunch of new orders have been placed in a dashboard like a list. then see the details (each detail will have a copy icon.)
    - Then manually we will contact some other service to handle the payment and delivery. (and mark the order as "Placed To Drop Shipping" in the dashboard.) (we'll call this manual process "Drop Shipping Process" and will be handled offline by the seller.)
    - now this Drop Shipping Process may or may not be automatic too. If I find a way or api of the drop shipping service to place orders then I might have a button with each order item "Place Order to Drop Shipping" and when clicked it will send the order details to the drop shipping service and mark the order as "Placed To Drop Shipping" in the dashboard.
    - In future we might add products other than drop shipping products, so the app should be able to handle both types of products (drop shipping and regular delivery products).
    - Keep multiple sources of products in mind. The app should be able to handle products from different sources and display them accordingly. So I can work with multiple drop shipping companies and also have my own products in the same app. (so the app should be able to handle both types of products and display them accordingly.)
    - from admin panel I'll select what products brand etc to show on the app. (so the admin can select what products to show on the app and what products to hide.)

    - Doo make it a bit elegant and modern, but not too flashy. so that the customers knows that we are not cheap or fake. (so that the customers can trust us and feel comfortable to place orders.)
